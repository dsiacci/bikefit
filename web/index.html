How to film
  1. Phone on a tripod, landscape, main lens (1×), 1080p at 60 frames per second if you can.
  2. Side view from the drive side, 2 to 3 metres away, lens at hip height, square to the bike.
  3. The whole bike and you in the frame, head included: the model finds people by their face.
  4. Close-fitting clothes, light in front of you, not behind.
  5. About a minute of steady pedalling, seated, easy effort, hands where they usually are.

What you get

  • How much your knee is still bent at the bottom of the stroke, stroke by stroke: the angle the knee-angle methods for saddle height look at.
  • Knee, hip and ankle through the whole stroke, trunk, shoulder and elbow angles.
  • The comparison with published ranges, with the source of each range.
  • The saddle change in millimetres that the leg geometry suggests.
  • A report to download and keep, or to bring to a bike fitter.

How the video was read

    What this can and cannot tell you

    • Side view only: nothing about how your knee tracks seen from the front, or about your feet and cleats.
    • A 2D pose model on a phone video is accurate to a few degrees, not to one degree.
    • Published ranges describe groups of riders. They are a starting point, not a target for you.
    • The result is valid for the bike in the video, set up as it was.
    • This is not medical advice. If something hurts, see a professional.
